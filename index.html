How Taskify Uses Your Google Calendar Data

Taskify syncs user's tasks with their Google Calendar. When a user creates a task with a due date, it automatically creates a corresponding event in their Google Calendar for better time management.

What We Access:

  • View and edit events on all your calendars (calendar.events scope)

Why We Need This Access:

  • Task-to-Calendar Sync: When you create a task in Taskify, we create a matching event in your Google Calendar
  • Bi-directional Updates: Changes to tasks in Taskify update the calendar events, and vice versa
  • Better Organization: See your tasks alongside your other calendar events for complete schedule visibility

Your Privacy Matters: We only access calendar data that you explicitly authorize. Your data is used solely for syncing tasks with your calendar and is never shared with third parties.
